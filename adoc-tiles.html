Briefing

The panel on the right overlays the ADOC crest with a grid of 1,000 numbered messages, left by whoever's passed through. Tap "+ Leave a Message" in the header to add your own.

  1. Scan the crest for a highlighted message.
  2. Click or tap any message to open it.
  3. Each entry shows the message's index, its contents, and its author.
  4. Posting a message assigns it a random open index.

The Challenge

Ten tiles somewhere in this crest hide a secret message. They look exactly like every other tile — no glow, no marker, nothing to give them away. Can you find all ten? Every one you open gets a small gold mark on it and a tick in the counter below, so you'll always know how close you are.
